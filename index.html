Daniel Good

Multidisciplinary artist and poet, who makes his home on the traditional lands of lək̓ʷəŋən speaking peoples, today known as the Esquimalt and Songhees Nations (Victoria, BC).

His work takes many forms, including collage, portraiture, sonnets, and free verse, and has grown from the interstices of his Jewish and Sḵwx̱wú7mesh heritage.
